# Changelog

What changed each night, newest first.

## Night 6 — 2026-10-29 — Friends come home
Pale, grey spirits now wander the faded land, sad and lost (look for soft dots on your map, or a shimmer in the dark).
Walk up to one and it takes hold of your light — lead it into the colour around a lantern, beacon or the Heart Stone
and it makes a home there. Friends stroll about, visit things they love (flower beds, benches, workbenches, paths, lanterns),
sleep beside their light at night, and chat when you click them — with a little gift each day and hints about where other
lost spirits are waiting. A new "Returning friends" chapter of goals guides the way.

## Night 5 — 2026-10-28 — Rain, wind and rainbows
The sky now has moods that come and go on their own: clear days with cloud shadows gliding over
the land, breezy spells that bend the trees and blow leaves and petals past, grey overcast skies,
soft drizzle and pouring rain (with splashes, ripples on lakes and a pattering soundscape), and
quiet rolling fog where lantern light glows wider. Rain helps plants grow back faster, butterflies
and rabbits shelter while it falls — but little frogs hop out to play — and when the rain clears on
a sunny day, a rainbow arcs across the sky. A new "Sky & weather" chapter of goals celebrates it all.

## Night 4 — 2026-10-27 — The workshop
Reeds now grow by shores and in meadows, and blue crystals glow in the grey highlands. Build a
Workbench, and while you stand near it you can craft an Axe (more wood) and a Pick (more stone,
and it lets you mine crystal) — then raise a Crystal Beacon that brings colour back to a huge area.
Decorate with fences that join together, glowing flower beds and benches, and press X to pick any
building back up and get its materials back. A new set of "Workshop" goals guides you through it all.

## Night 3 — 2026-10-26 — Life returns
Restored land now fills with life: butterflies flutter and rest on flowers, songbirds glide
in and peck the grass (walk too close and the whole flock takes off), rabbits hop about and
dash away from you, then come back. Fish swim in coloured water and sometimes leap with a splash,
moths dance around lanterns at night, and water ripples as you walk along the shore.
Colour now fades in softly at the edge of a lantern's glow instead of in square blocks,
and there's a new goal: meet three kinds of returning wildlife.

## Night 2 — 2026-10-24 — Finding your way
A round map now sits in the top-right corner and fills in as you wander — grey where
the world is faded, green and bright around your lanterns. Press M (or tap it) to open
the big map: drag to look around, scroll to zoom.
New players get a "First steps" tracker that teaches the game one small goal at a time,
with a happy chime and little gifts of materials when each one is done.

## Night 1 — 2026-10-23 — The world finds its voice
Lumen now has sound, all made live in code. Chopping wood goes "tok", stones clink,
petals sparkle, and lighting a lantern rings a rising chime. Footsteps sound different
on grass, leaves, sand, stone and bridges.
Listen to the world change: grey land has a lonely wind, restored land fills with
birdsong and a warm chord by day, crickets and owls at night, and waves near water.
Press N (or the speaker icon) to turn sound on or off.

## Night 0 — 2026-10-23 — A faded world
The beginning. An endless world of meadows, forests, lakes and highlands, all faded
to grey except around the glowing Heart Stone. Gather wood, stone and glow petals,
craft lanterns that bring colour back, lay stone paths and build bridges.
Day turns to night, flowers and lanterns glow in the dark, and your progress saves.
