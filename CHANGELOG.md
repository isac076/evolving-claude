# Changelog

What changed each night, newest first.

## Night 21 — 2026-11-13 — Softer edges and a calmer option
A review night. Where two kinds of land meet — the violet Glowcap Hollows and the meadow, grass and sand, forest and field — the
border is now soft and wavy instead of a hard staircase, and the edge of a lantern's colour fades smoothly (the game also draws
faster). Names on the big map no longer pile on top of each other, the ending page fits on a phone, and the Resting menu has a new
"Calm motion" switch for less swaying, drifting and flashing.

## Night 20 — 2026-11-12 — The Great Sun
The story now has an ending. Far beyond the last Ember Shrine stands the Sun's Hearth, a great golden dais with a cold altar
and four flame-shaped sockets — every shrine you kindle lights one. Once all four burn, a golden arrow leads you there: press E
at the altar and the four embers gather, the Great Sun rises, night turns to dawn and colour wakes softly all over the world,
even far from your lanterns. A warm ending page tells your journey in numbers — then you keep playing in a sunnier world.

## Night 19 — 2026-11-11 — The hush of night
On dark nights, out in the grey far from your lanterns, little hushlings rise out of the dark — soft shadows with pale eyes,
left over from the Fading. They drift after you, hold on, and sip your glow (your light grows small, but never goes out). They
are never fought: lead them back into a lantern's light, or press F to Shine, and they turn into starlight. Three starlight make
a Moon lantern that keeps them far away, and a workbench can weave a Starlit charm that protects your light.

## Night 18 — 2026-11-10 — Riverbanks & waymarks
Soft reddish clay now lies where shallow water meets a beach or meadow — dig it with E (the goal tracker points the way).
Clay makes stepping stones you can lay across the shallows and hop over, and signposts: put one up and type a name for
the place — it floats over the post when you walk by, shows on your map and journal, and nearby lanterns take the name on the
lantern roads ("Lantern at Otter Ford"). Bright banners stream in the wind (click one to change its colour), and a workbench makes clay flower planters.

## Night 17 — 2026-11-09 — The Glowcap Hollows
A brand-new kind of land: deep, damp hollows of violet moss where giant mushrooms grow and little glowcaps kept their light
all through the Fading — at night they shine in the dark and glowing spores drift up into the air. Every world has one a short
walk from the Heart Stone (the goal tracker points the way). Gather glow spores to build a teal Mushroom lamp, plant spore
patches that grow into glowcaps, and once the colour is back, look for shy glow snails leaving shiny trails.

## Night 16 — 2026-11-08 — The lantern roads
Your lights are now joined by old roads of light. Stand beside any lantern, open the map (M) and golden threads run to
every other light you've lit — click one and your spirit leaps into the sky as a bright mote, flies along a curving road
over the land (any friends following you come too) and lands softly beside it in a ring of light. Beacons, ember lanterns,
woken memory stones and kindled shrines are stops as well, so the far-off Ember Shrines are now just a flight away.

## Night 15 — 2026-11-07 — The Ember Shrines
Far out in the faded land stand four great Ember Shrines. Wake three memory stones and they appear on your map, with a
soft ember arrow pointing the way. Each shrine has four ember bowls that ask for an offering (wood, petals, crystal…);
light all four and embers leap into the great hearth, it bursts into flame, and a wave of colour rolls out across the land
as far as you can see. At night a kindled shrine sends a beam of light into the sky, and the first one teaches you the Ember lantern.

## Night 14 — 2026-11-06 — A tidy-up and foamy shores
A review night. Coasts now have a soft line of white foam that gently breathes along the curving shore, with a faint
wave rolling in and out. The goal tracker no longer overflows once every goal is done (one glowing pip per chapter), the
minimap steps aside while you trade at a market, and friends chatting far off screen no longer leave a stray bubble in a corner.

## Night 13 — 2026-11-05 — The village grows
When two friends' cottages stand close together, the friends build a little hamlet with its own name and a stone well
(press E at the well to water every garden around it). A third home brings a market stall where you can swap things you
have plenty of, and a fourth raises a ribbon pole on the village green. At dusk neighbours meet at the well to chat, and on
the last evening of every season (and the night the pole goes up) the village holds a festival: everyone dances in a ring,
bunting glows between the roofs, paper lanterns float into the night sky and the music turns into a lively waltz.

## Night 12 — 2026-11-04 — The turning seasons
The year now turns every few days. Spring brings pink blossom and fresh green grass; summer is lush and golden with
fireflies at night; in autumn the trees go gold, orange and red and leaves drift down through the mist; and in winter
snow covers the land, leafy trees stand bare, rain falls as soft snowflakes, you leave footprints, rabbits wear white
coats and your friends put on scarves. The season shows at the top of the screen, gardens grow fastest in spring, and
friends have something to say about each season.

## Night 11 — 2026-11-03 — Ruins of the old world
The faded land is now dotted with ruins: stone circles, fallen gates you can walk through, dry fountains, a hooded keeper
statue and broken halls of lanterns. At the heart of each sleeps a carved memory stone — stand beside it and press E to wake
it: it glows gold, colour returns around the ruin (the fountain runs again, the keeper's lantern lights up at night) and you
read one memory of the story of why the world faded. A new Journal (J, or the little book at the top) keeps every memory,
the ruins you've found, the wildlife you've met and your friends, and friends sometimes remember which way the old stones lie.

## Night 10 — 2026-11-02 — Wishes and cottages
Your friends now make wishes! A little heart bubble over a friend means they're wishing for something — talk to them to
hear it: a few glow petals, a flower bed or a path near their home, a new lantern, another lost spirit brought home…
Wishes appear in a new list on the left, and the heart turns gold when a wish can come true. Each one earns a gift and a
friendship heart (close friends give bigger daily gifts), and your first granted wish teaches you to build a Cottage:
a friend whose light is close by moves in, goes inside to sleep at night with the window glowing warm, and has smoke
curling from the chimney at dusk. A new "Friends' wishes" chapter of goals guides the way.

## Night 9 — 2026-11-01 — The world sings
Lumen now has music that writes itself as you play — a new little tune every minute or so. In grey land you hear
only a few lonely notes; walk into colour and a soft bass, a melody and a high sparkle bloom in. Mornings, sunny days,
dusk, rain and fog each have their own mood, nights bring a slow music-box lullaby, and the ground picks the
instrument (a flute in meadows, marimba in the woods, kalimba on the shore, glassy bells in the highlands).
Friends who are awake nearby join in — singers sing, gardeners pluck a harp, tinkerers tap the beat, dreamers ring
chimes, wanderers play a pipe — with little glowing notes floating up. The name of each piece appears softly in the
corner, there's a Music slider in the Resting menu, and a new "Song of the world" chapter of goals.

## Night 8 — 2026-10-31 — Seeds and gardens
Glow blooms now often drop flower seeds and trees drop acorns. Plant them from the new Garden group in the
craft menu and watch them grow, stage by stage, into real blooms and tall trees — fastest in coloured land and
damp soil. Make a Watering Can at the workbench (press E beside a dry sprout), or let the rain water every garden
at once. Some seeds become rare moonflowers that open and shine at night, and anything you gather now visibly
grows back: chopped trees leave a stump that sprouts a new little tree.

## Night 7 — 2026-10-30 — A quiet place to rest
A review night. Press Esc (or the little moon by the sound button) to open the new Resting menu: the world
pauses, and you can set the volume and world sounds, turn on larger text, read how to play, go back to the
title or start a new world. Coastlines and the edges of sand, forest and highlands now curve softly instead of
stepping in squares, the game runs about twice as smoothly, phones get a tidier screen, and the spirit no
longer keeps walking by itself if you switch windows mid-step.

## Night 6 — 2026-10-29 — Friends come home
Pale, grey spirits now wander the faded land, sad and lost (look for soft dots on your map, or a shimmer in the dark).
Walk up to one and it takes hold of your light — lead it into the colour around a lantern, beacon or the Heart Stone
and it makes a home there. Friends stroll about, visit things they love (flower beds, benches, workbenches, paths, lanterns),
sleep beside their light at night, and chat when you click them — with a little gift each day and hints about where other
lost spirits are waiting. A new "Returning friends" chapter of goals guides the way.

## Night 5 — 2026-10-28 — Rain, wind and rainbows
The sky now has moods that come and go on their own: clear days with cloud shadows gliding over
the land, breezy spells that bend the trees and blow leaves and petals past, grey overcast skies,
soft drizzle and pouring rain (with splashes, ripples on lakes and a pattering soundscape), and
quiet rolling fog where lantern light glows wider. Rain helps plants grow back faster, butterflies
and rabbits shelter while it falls — but little frogs hop out to play — and when the rain clears on
a sunny day, a rainbow arcs across the sky. A new "Sky & weather" chapter of goals celebrates it all.

## Night 4 — 2026-10-27 — The workshop
Reeds now grow by shores and in meadows, and blue crystals glow in the grey highlands. Build a
Workbench, and while you stand near it you can craft an Axe (more wood) and a Pick (more stone,
and it lets you mine crystal) — then raise a Crystal Beacon that brings colour back to a huge area.
Decorate with fences that join together, glowing flower beds and benches, and press X to pick any
building back up and get its materials back. A new set of "Workshop" goals guides you through it all.

## Night 3 — 2026-10-26 — Life returns
Restored land now fills with life: butterflies flutter and rest on flowers, songbirds glide
in and peck the grass (walk too close and the whole flock takes off), rabbits hop about and
dash away from you, then come back. Fish swim in coloured water and sometimes leap with a splash,
moths dance around lanterns at night, and water ripples as you walk along the shore.
Colour now fades in softly at the edge of a lantern's glow instead of in square blocks,
and there's a new goal: meet three kinds of returning wildlife.

## Night 2 — 2026-10-24 — Finding your way
A round map now sits in the top-right corner and fills in as you wander — grey where
the world is faded, green and bright around your lanterns. Press M (or tap it) to open
the big map: drag to look around, scroll to zoom.
New players get a "First steps" tracker that teaches the game one small goal at a time,
with a happy chime and little gifts of materials when each one is done.

## Night 1 — 2026-10-23 — The world finds its voice
Lumen now has sound, all made live in code. Chopping wood goes "tok", stones clink,
petals sparkle, and lighting a lantern rings a rising chime. Footsteps sound different
on grass, leaves, sand, stone and bridges.
Listen to the world change: grey land has a lonely wind, restored land fills with
birdsong and a warm chord by day, crickets and owls at night, and waves near water.
Press N (or the speaker icon) to turn sound on or off.

## Night 0 — 2026-10-23 — A faded world
The beginning. An endless world of meadows, forests, lakes and highlands, all faded
to grey except around the glowing Heart Stone. Gather wood, stone and glow petals,
craft lanterns that bring colour back, lay stone paths and build bridges.
Day turns to night, flowers and lanterns glow in the dark, and your progress saves.
