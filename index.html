Lanterns bring the colour back. Lost spirits follow your light — lead them into the colour and they make a home. A heart over a friend means they have a wish: talk to them to hear it. Cottages built close together grow into a village with a well, a market and festivals. Old ruins hide memory stones — wake them to learn why the world faded. The seasons turn every few days: blossom, summer, falling leaves and snow.
